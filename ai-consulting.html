// What it is

What is AI consulting?

AI consulting is expert guidance that helps a business decide where AI will create real value and how to capture it. Done well, it cuts through the noise. Instead of chasing whatever is fashionable, you get a clear-eyed look at your own operations, a ranked list of where AI will actually pay off, and a practical plan to get there.

The trouble with most AI advice is that it stops at advice. A deck of recommendations is easy to produce and easy to ignore, and the gap between strategy and a working system is where most AI ambitions quietly die. The hard, valuable part is turning a plan into software that runs in your business and moves a number.

That is the gap CodimAI is built to close. We consult and we build. The engagement starts with an audit and a costed roadmap, and continues, if you choose, into building and deploying the agents we recommend. You are never handed a strategy and left to find someone to execute it. The advice and the delivery come from the same team that will stand behind the result.

// Advisors who build

Why builders make better advisors

There is a reason our advice is grounded: we live with the consequences of it. When the team writing your roadmap is the same team that will build it, the recommendations change. They get specific, honest about effort, and ruthless about what will actually work, because vague advice becomes our problem at build time.

A pure consultancy has no such pressure. It can recommend the ambitious, the trendy, or the impressive, hand over the deck, and move on. The plan looks great and stalls the moment someone has to implement it against real data and real systems. The cost of that gap lands on you.

Because we build, our roadmaps are sequenced for reality. We push the quick, high-return wins to the front so you see value early and fund the rest from results. We are honest about where your data needs work before automation will pay off. And we choose tools and models on merit, not loyalty, because we have no product to push, only an outcome to deliver.

That alignment, advice you can hold us to, is the whole point. Strategy is only as good as what it becomes. Ours becomes software that works.

// Getting started

Start with the free audit

The entire engagement begins with one low-commitment step: a free AI audit. You do not need a strategy, a budget, or clean data to take it. You just need a willingness to look honestly at where your team spends its time.

In the audit, we learn how your business runs, identify where AI will create the most value, and model the return. You come away with a prioritised, costed roadmap, a document that turns a vague sense that you should be doing something with AI into a concrete plan with numbers attached. It is yours whether or not you build with us.

Most clients move ahead, because once the return is on paper the decision makes itself. We build the first agent, prove it, and work down the roadmap from there. But there is no pressure and no lock-in. The audit's job is simply to give you clarity, and clarity, it turns out, is most of what AI strategy has been missing.

Think of the audit as a free second opinion on your own operations from a team that builds AI for a living. Even if you do nothing with it for six months, you will have a documented, costed view of where the opportunities are, ready for when the timing is right. There is very little downside to knowing, and a real cost to guessing, so the sensible first move is simply to look.

// Why CodimAI

Consulting without the guesswork

The hardest thing about adopting AI is not the technology. It is deciding what to do, in what order, with confidence that the effort will pay back. Most businesses know AI could help somewhere; few have a clear, evidence-based view of where. Our consulting exists to give you exactly that, quickly and without the usual fog of jargon and hype.

We keep the engagement honest because we have skin in the game. We do not bill by the hour to produce a thick report; we model the return and stake our recommendation on building it. That changes the incentives entirely. We have no reason to overstate what AI can do, because we are the ones who have to deliver it. The plan you get is the plan we would build, costed the way we would build it.

We are also vendor-neutral. We are not reselling a platform, so we are free to recommend the right tool, the right model, or, just as often, the conclusion that a particular workflow is not worth automating yet. That independence is rare and valuable. It means the advice is genuinely on your side, optimised for your return rather than someone's licence revenue.

And we sequence for momentum. The fastest, highest-confidence wins go first, so you see results in weeks and fund the rest from the savings they create. Instead of a daunting transformation programme, you get a series of small, proven steps, each one lower risk because the last one worked. That is how AI adoption should feel: clear, paced, and paying for itself as it goes.

By the end of the first conversation you will know more about where AI fits in your business than most companies learn in a year of pilots. That clarity is the real product of good AI consulting, and it is where everything else begins.

It is worth being clear about what we do not do. We do not sell AI as a magic fix, recommend automating things that work fine by hand, or push a transformation bigger than your business needs. Plenty of work should stay with people, and a good advisor will tell you so. Our job is to find the specific places where AI genuinely beats the status quo, and to be honest about the rest.

That honesty is what makes the relationship last. The first agent is rarely the last, because once a team sees one repetitive burden lifted, they start spotting others. Clients come back not because they are locked in, but because the first engagement paid off and the next opportunity is obvious. A roadmap that delivers earns the right to the next chapter.

// FAQ

AI consulting questions

What is AI consulting?

AI consulting is expert guidance that helps a business decide where and how to use AI. It covers auditing your workflows, identifying the highest-return opportunities, estimating the ROI, and producing a practical roadmap, so you invest in AI that pays off rather than AI for its own sake.

What does an AI consulting engagement include?

A typical engagement starts with a free audit of your processes, followed by a prioritised list of AI opportunities ranked by return and effort, an ROI estimate, and a roadmap. From there we can build and deploy the agents and systems we recommend, so strategy turns into working software.

How is CodimAI different from a traditional consultancy?

We are builders, not just advisors. A traditional consultancy hands you a slide deck. We deliver a costed plan and then build the agents that execute it, so you get results, not recommendations that sit on a shelf.

Do we need technical staff or clean data to start?

No. The audit meets you where you are. We assess your data and systems as part of the engagement and design a plan that fits your reality, including any groundwork needed before automation.

How much does AI consulting cost?

The initial audit and ROI assessment are free. Beyond that, scope depends on what you choose to build. Because the audit models the return up front, you can decide what to fund based on expected payback.

How long does the audit take?

The audit is fast, usually a short discovery conversation followed by a written report within days. It is designed to give you a clear, actionable picture quickly, not to drag out.

What if we are not ready to build yet?

That is fine. The roadmap is yours to keep and act on when you choose. There is no obligation to build with us, though most clients move ahead because the plan makes the return concrete.
