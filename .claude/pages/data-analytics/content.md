## FAQ

**Q1: What data sources can CodimAI connect to?**
CodimAI integrates with CRMs, ERPs, e-commerce platforms, databases, spreadsheets, and third-party APIs. The data audit identifies your specific sources and any gaps before development begins. We do not require a clean data warehouse to start.

**Q2: How long does a data analytics engagement take?**
A typical engagement runs eight to sixteen weeks from initial audit to first production dashboard. Timeline depends on data complexity, the number of integrations, and how clearly the KPIs are defined at the outset.

**Q3: Do we need a data team in-house to work with CodimAI?**
No. CodimAI provides end-to-end delivery including data engineering, modelling, and dashboard development. A business stakeholder who can define what good decisions look like is enough to start.

**Q4: How is this different from a standard BI tool like Power BI or Tableau?**
Standard BI tools provide visualisation infrastructure  you still need to build the data model, define KPIs, and maintain pipelines. CodimAI delivers the full intelligence layer: the logic, the automation, and the ongoing optimisation. The output can surface in any interface, including existing BI tools.

**Q5: How does CodimAI measure the ROI of an analytics project?**
We define ROI metrics during the audit phase  time saved on reporting, decisions made faster, revenue attributed to insight-driven actions. After deployment, we track these directly and report on them as part of the continuous improvement cycle.

**Q6: Can CodimAI work with our existing dashboards and reports?**
Yes. We start with an audit of what exists, what is used, and what is trusted. We build on working foundations where possible and replace only what is genuinely broken or missing.
