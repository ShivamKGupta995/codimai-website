## FAQ

**Q1: What is agentic AI and how does it differ from standard AI tools?**
A1: Agentic AI refers to systems that can plan multi-step tasks, use tools, verify their own outputs, and complete complex work autonomously. Unlike standard AI tools that respond to a single prompt, agentic systems handle entire workflows end-to-end — reducing the need for constant human intervention at each step.

**Q2: What does the free Business & ROI Audit involve?**
A2: CodimAI's audit maps your current workflows and identifies processes where agentic AI can reduce time, cost, or error rate. We then model the financial impact of each opportunity so you can prioritise clearly. The audit takes one to two weeks and is delivered as a written report with specific recommendations.

**Q3: Do you work with businesses that have no existing AI infrastructure?**
A3: Yes. Most clients start from legacy systems and limited data pipelines. We assess what exists, design a practical roadmap, and often begin with a single focused use case before expanding. No AI experience is required on your team.

**Q4: How long does a typical agentic AI project take?**
A4: Scope varies by complexity. A focused agentic workflow — such as an automated review responder or a research agent — can deploy in six to ten weeks. Larger orchestration systems typically take three to five months. The ROI Audit and strategy phase clarifies the full scope before any development begins.

**Q5: What industries does CodimAI serve?**
A5: CodimAI works across e-commerce, professional services, hospitality, retail, and B2B SaaS. The agentic AI process adapts to any industry where there are repeatable decisions, large communication volumes, or high-cost manual work.

**Q6: What happens after the agentic AI is deployed?**
A6: CodimAI provides ongoing monitoring, performance tracking against the original ROI model, and periodic retraining as your data and processes evolve. Agentic systems improve with use — we make sure yours does.
