## FAQ
**Q1: What is a WhatsApp AI agent?**
A1: A WhatsApp AI agent is an automated assistant that handles customer conversations on WhatsApp  answering questions, qualifying leads, sending messages, and resolving requests end-to-end. CodimAI's agent is grounded in your business systems so its replies are accurate, and it escalates to a human when needed.

**Q2: Can it send unlimited messages?**
A2: Yes. It runs on the official WhatsApp Business Platform, so you can send broadcasts, alerts, and campaigns at any scale. Volume follows Meta's standard messaging and quality rules, which we set up and manage for you.

**Q3: Can I build chatbot flows and forms without code?**
A3: Yes. A visual builder lets you design guided conversations, branching logic, and in-chat forms by connecting steps  no developers required. Each step can call your systems, so a completed form lands directly in your CRM.

**Q4: Does it integrate with my existing tools?**
A4: It integrates with any application that has an API  CRM, e-commerce, helpdesk, or internal dashboards. It reads and writes real data, so orders sync, tickets open, and leads route automatically from inside the chat.

**Q5: Can customers browse products inside WhatsApp?**
A5: Yes. Your product catalog appears as rich cards inside the chat, so customers browse, ask questions, and buy without leaving WhatsApp.

**Q6: Does it use the official WhatsApp Business API?**
A6: Yes. CodimAI deploys on the official WhatsApp Business Platform, which keeps your account compliant and your messaging reliable at scale, and always hands off cleanly to a human when needed.
