// FAQ

Common questions

What does an email AI agent do?

An email AI agent reads incoming messages in context, triages them by intent and urgency, and drafts accurate replies in your tone. CodimAI's agent clears routine email and escalates anything sensitive or ambiguous to a person.

Does it send emails automatically?

Only if you want it to. By default the agent prepares drafts for human review; you choose which categories of email it is allowed to send autonomously.

How does it match our tone of voice?

We ground the agent in your previous replies and approved content, so drafts read like your team wrote them and stay consistent with your brand.

Which email systems does it work with?

The agent integrates with common business email providers and helpdesk tools. The free audit confirms compatibility with your specific stack.
