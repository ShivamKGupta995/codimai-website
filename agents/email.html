// Follow-up automation

Sequences that know when to stop.

Most deals need four to seven touches before they convert. The agent handles every one of them and stops the moment the contact responds, so you never come across as a machine.

  1. Day 0

    Personalized first touch

    The opening email is written from scratch for each contact referencing their company, their role, and the specific reason you're reaching out. No variables, no obvious merges.

  2. Day 3

    Different angle, same voice

    If no reply, the agent sends a follow-up that shifts the framing leading with a relevant insight, a case study, or a specific benefit without repeating the first email.

  3. Day 7

    Low-friction re-engagement

    The third touch is lighter a short, direct question that's easy to answer. It lowers the barrier to a reply and opens a conversation without pressure.

  4. Reply

    Sequence stops instantly

    The moment the contact replies, the sequence halts and the reply is routed to the right person with full context. The agent never chases someone who's already in a conversation.

// Campaign management

From segment to sent in minutes.

Define a target segment, review the generated copy, approve and go. The agent writes a unique message for every contact in the list and handles delivery, timing, and tracking end-to-end.

  • Segment

    CRM-driven targeting

    Build your audience with simple CRM filters: industry, deal stage, last reply date, product purchased. No exports, no spreadsheets the agent reads your CRM directly.

  • Copy

    Reviewed before it sends

    The agent drafts the campaign copy for your approval you see a representative sample, edit the tone or message if needed, and then authorize the send. Human judgement stays in the loop.

  • Send

    Personalized at contact level

    Even within a campaign, each email is individually tailored the introduction, the specific benefit highlighted, and the call to action all adapt to that contact's context.

  • Measure

    Results by contact and segment

    Opens, clicks, replies, and conversions are tracked at the individual level and rolled up into campaign-level insights, so you know exactly what's working and why.

// FAQ

Common questions

What does the AI email agent do?

The CodimAI email agent writes personalized outreach and follow-up emails for each contact using their name, company, purchase history, and context from your CRM then schedules and sends them automatically. It handles the whole sequence: first touch, follow-ups, and re-engagement, all in your voice.

How does it personalize emails at scale?

It pulls contact data from your CRM name, company, past interactions, product interests and constructs a unique message for each recipient. Every email reads like it was written personally for that one person, not merged from a template.

Can it set up automated follow-up sequences?

Yes. You define the sequence logic for example, follow up on day 3 if no reply, then again on day 7 with a different angle and the agent handles the timing and writing automatically. It pauses the sequence the moment someone replies.

Can I run campaigns to a segment of clients?

Yes. Build a segment from CRM filters (industry, deal stage, last activity), approve the campaign copy, and the agent sends personalized messages to every contact in the segment with individual tailoring, not mass blast.

Does it integrate with Gmail, Outlook, or our CRM?

The agent integrates with common business email providers and CRM platforms. The free audit confirms compatibility with your specific stack and maps the data connections needed.

What are the open rate and conversion improvements?

Highly personalized emails consistently outperform bulk sends. Clients using the CodimAI email agent typically see open rates rise to 55–70% and reply rates of 25–40%, compared to 15–25% for generic campaigns.
