// Reviews & local SEO

Your review profile is a ranking signal.

Google's local algorithm treats review quality, recency, volume, and response rate as direct ranking factors. Every reply the agent writes is a micro-investment in your local search position.

  • Signal 1

    Response rate

    Businesses that reply to the majority of their reviews rank higher in local pack results. The agent replies to every review often within minutes making your response rate near 100%.

  • Signal 2

    Review recency & volume

    The review request campaign drives a steady stream of fresh reviews, which Google interprets as active customer engagement improving your local relevance score continuously.

  • Signal 3

    Keyword density in replies

    Replies mention your services, location, and specialisms naturally. Over hundreds of replies, this builds a keyword-rich profile that reinforces what you rank for in local searches.

  • Signal 4

    Rating maintenance

    Holding a 4.5–5 star average influences Google's perception of your authority and customer trust, both of which feed into local pack ranking and the conversion rate once visitors reach your profile.

// Negative review recovery

Turn a bad review into a visible resolution.

A well-handled negative review often impresses future customers more than a perfect score. Seeing a business acknowledge and fix a problem builds more trust than reading only five-star praise.

  1. 01

    Acknowledge immediately

    The agent drafts an empathetic, non-defensive reply within minutes acknowledging the experience specifically, not with a generic apology.

  2. 02

    Move the conversation offline

    The reply invites the reviewer to contact you directly to resolve the issue taking a constructive step without airing details publicly.

  3. 03

    Trigger the service-recovery flow

    The agent can simultaneously send a direct message (via email or WhatsApp) to the reviewer if their contact details are in your system proactively resolving the issue.

// FAQ

Common questions

How does the agent keep ratings between 4.5 and 5 stars?

The agent monitors your review profile in real time. When a low-star review arrives, it flags it immediately for a human response, drafts a constructive and empathetic reply, and can trigger a service-recovery flow to contact the customer directly turning a negative into a resolved issue that future readers see as handled professionally.

Does replying to reviews actually improve Google ranking?

Yes. Google's local ranking algorithm considers review engagement the freshness, volume, and quality of responses. Businesses that reply consistently and quickly rank higher in local pack results. The agent also weaves relevant keywords naturally into replies, compounding the SEO benefit.

Will the replies sound genuine or templated?

Every reply is written from scratch for that specific review referencing what the reviewer said, their specific experience, and your brand voice. Generic templates are the opposite of what the agent is built to produce.

Can it handle reviews across multiple locations?

Yes. The agent can monitor and reply across multiple Google Business Profiles simultaneously, each with its own voice, location-specific context, and manager routing. The free audit covers your full profile footprint.

What happens with a seriously negative or legal review?

Any review flagged as sensitive legal risk, severe complaint, or a claim that needs verification is immediately escalated to a human and never replied to automatically. The agent always errs on the side of caution on anything that could cause reputational or legal exposure.

Can we ask happy customers to leave reviews?

Yes. The agent can trigger a post-transaction review request via email or WhatsApp at the right moment when satisfaction is highest using a compliant, non-incentivized message. This lifts review volume organically and keeps the rating climbing.
