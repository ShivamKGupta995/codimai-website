// FAQ

Common questions

What does a Google Review AI agent do?

A Google Review AI agent reads each new review, interprets its sentiment, and drafts a personalised, brand-safe reply. CodimAI's agent ensures every review is answered promptly and consistently, with human oversight on sensitive cases.

Why does replying to reviews quickly matter?

A timely, thoughtful reply signals attentiveness to prospective customers reading your profile, and responsiveness is a factor in local search visibility. The first 48 hours carry the most weight.

Will the replies sound generic?

No. Each response is tailored to the specific review and written to your brand voice. Generic, copy-paste replies are exactly what the agent is built to avoid.

Can we approve replies before they post?

Yes. You decide whether the agent posts automatically, posts only to positive reviews, or routes every draft for human approval first.
