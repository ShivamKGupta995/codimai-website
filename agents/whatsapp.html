// FAQ

Common questions

What is a WhatsApp AI agent?

A WhatsApp AI agent is an automated assistant that handles customer conversations on WhatsApp — answering questions, qualifying leads, and resolving requests end-to-end. CodimAI's agent is grounded in your business systems so its replies are accurate, and it escalates to a human when needed.

Does it use the official WhatsApp Business API?

Yes. CodimAI deploys on the official WhatsApp Business Platform, which keeps your account compliant and your messaging reliable at scale.

Can it hand off to a human agent?

Always. You define exactly when the agent escalates, and the full conversation transfers with it so customers never have to repeat themselves.

Will it sound like our brand?

Yes. The agent replies in your tone of voice and is grounded in your approved content, so conversations stay consistent and on-brand.
