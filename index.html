FAQ

Questions, answered.

How long does implementation take?

Most agents go live in two to four weeks. We start with a free audit, build a working prototype, validate it against your data, then deploy to production.

What systems can you integrate?

WhatsApp, email, CRMs, helpdesks, spreadsheets, databases, and most tools with an API. If it has an interface, an agent can usually work with it.

Do you use OpenAI?

We are model-agnostic. We use the best model for each task, including Claude, OpenAI, and open models, and can run on your preferred provider.

Can agents access company data?

Yes. Agents work over your documents, records, and systems with permission-aware access. Your data stays yours and is never used to train shared models.

What ROI can we expect?

It depends on the process, but clients typically automate 60 to 80 percent of a repetitive workflow. The free audit gives you a modelled estimate before any build.

Do you provide ongoing support?

Yes. We monitor, tune, and improve every agent after launch, and add new capabilities as your needs grow.

Can agents work on WhatsApp?

Yes. Our WhatsApp agent answers, qualifies, and resolves conversations around the clock inside the chat your customers already use.

Can agents connect to our CRM?

Yes. Agents read and write to CRMs to qualify leads, log activity, update records, and book meetings automatically.
