// FAQ

Common questions

What is generative AI for business content?

Generative AI for business content refers to systems that produce written material — marketing copy, emails, reports, proposals, documentation — automatically from structured inputs or prompts. Unlike general AI writing tools, CodimAI's systems are trained and configured to match your brand voice, integrate with your existing workflows, and produce output that meets your specific quality and compliance standards.

How does generative AI maintain brand consistency?

Brand guidelines, tone examples, and style rules are encoded into the system at build time through fine-tuning and structured prompt engineering. Every piece of output is evaluated against these rules before it leaves the pipeline — not as a post-hoc check, but as a built-in constraint.

Can the system integrate with our CMS or marketing platform?

Yes. CodimAI builds integration layers that connect the generative system to your existing tools — including common CMS platforms, CRM systems, email marketing tools, and document management systems. We assess integration requirements during the strategy phase.

What human review is still needed after deployment?

The amount of human review depends on the content type and risk level. High-volume, lower-risk content such as product descriptions or support articles typically moves to near-automated publication. Higher-stakes content such as proposals or executive communications routes through an approval step. We design the right workflow for each content category during strategy.

How long does it take to deploy a generative AI content system?

A focused system for one content type — such as email generation or product descriptions — typically deploys in four to eight weeks. Broader multi-channel content platforms take two to four months. The ROI Audit clarifies scope before development begins.

Does the system improve over time?

Yes. CodimAI's generative systems include feedback loops that incorporate editor corrections, approval patterns, and performance data into ongoing model refinement. Output quality improves as the system learns your preferences.
