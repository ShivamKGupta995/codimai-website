// The Approach

How We Build Your Analytics Layer

CodimAI's analytics engagement starts with understanding the business — not the technology. Before a dashboard is built, we map your data sources and design an insight framework that matches how your organisation actually works. For narrative-form reporting, see Generative AI.

  1. 01

    Data Audit

    We inventory your existing data sources, assess quality and completeness, and identify what is missing or unreliable. No assumptions — only evidence.

  2. 02

    KPI Identification

    We work with your team to define the metrics that matter — not every available number, but the ones that signal business health and drive decisions.

  3. 03

    Insight Framework Design

    We design the logic layer: how data is aggregated, segmented, interpreted, and surfaced — before writing a line of code. The framework is the product.

  4. 04

    Dashboard & Analytics Development

    We build the reporting layer, integrate it with your data sources, and deliver interfaces your teams will actually use — not ones that require training to interpret.

  5. 05

    Continuous Improvement

    Analytics needs evolve. We monitor data quality, update models, and refine insights as your business changes — keeping the intelligence layer current.

// FAQ

Common questions

What data sources can CodimAI connect to?

CodimAI integrates with CRMs, ERPs, e-commerce platforms, databases, spreadsheets, and third-party APIs. The data audit identifies your specific sources and any gaps before development begins. We do not require a clean data warehouse to start.

How long does a data analytics engagement take?

A typical engagement runs eight to sixteen weeks from initial audit to first production dashboard. Timeline depends on data complexity, the number of integrations, and how clearly the KPIs are defined at the outset.

Do we need a data team in-house to work with CodimAI?

No. CodimAI provides end-to-end delivery including data engineering, modelling, and dashboard development. A business stakeholder who can define what good decisions look like is enough to start.

How is this different from a standard BI tool like Power BI or Tableau?

Standard BI tools provide visualisation infrastructure — you still need to build the data model, define KPIs, and maintain pipelines. CodimAI delivers the full intelligence layer: the logic, the automation, and the ongoing optimisation. The output can surface in any interface, including existing BI tools.

How does CodimAI measure the ROI of an analytics project?

We define ROI metrics during the audit phase — time saved on reporting, decisions made faster, revenue attributed to insight-driven actions. After deployment, we track these directly and report on them as part of the continuous improvement cycle.

Can CodimAI work with our existing dashboards and reports?

Yes. We start with an audit of what exists, what is used, and what is trusted. We build on working foundations where possible and replace only what is genuinely broken or missing.
