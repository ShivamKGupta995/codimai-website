// FAQ

Common questions

What is an AI recommendation system?

An AI recommendation system predicts what a user is most likely to want next — a product, piece of content, or action — by learning from behavioural data. CodimAI builds these systems tuned to your catalogue, customers, and business goals.

How accurate are the recommendations?

Accuracy depends on data quality and volume, but every CodimAI model is validated against your existing baseline through A/B testing. We deploy a model only when it demonstrably outperforms what you have today.

Can it respect business rules like stock and margin?

Yes. Recommendations are ranked within the constraints you define — inventory levels, margins, eligibility, and brand fit — so suggestions are always commercially sensible.

Do we need a large amount of data to start?

Not necessarily. We assess your behavioural data during the audit and choose an approach that fits — from content-based methods that work with a modest catalogue to collaborative models that improve as data accumulates.

Where do the recommendations appear?

Anywhere your users are — product pages, home feeds, email, search results, or in-app prompts. We deliver the ranking layer and integrate it with your existing front end through an API.

How long until we see results?

A focused recommendation engine typically deploys within six to ten weeks, with measurable lift visible during the first round of A/B testing.
