// FAQ

Common questions

What is AI-driven insight analysis?

AI-driven insight analysis uses machine learning to read across operational data, detect meaningful trends and anomalies, and explain them in plain language. Unlike a static dashboard, it surfaces what changed and why, so teams start from evidence rather than hunting through charts.

How is this different from a BI dashboard?

A dashboard shows metrics and waits for you to interpret them. CodimAI Insights does the interpretation — it ranks what matters, explains the likely drivers, and delivers findings as readable prose, reducing the time from data to decision.

What data sources can it connect to?

Insights connects to common data warehouses, product and operational databases, and flat files such as CSV or spreadsheet exports. Connections are read-only and scoped to the sources you explicitly approve.

Is our data kept private and secure?

Yes. Access is read-only, encrypted in transit, and limited to the data you authorise. We design every engagement around your security and compliance requirements from day one.
