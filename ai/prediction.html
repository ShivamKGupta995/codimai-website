// FAQ

Common questions

What can AI prediction models forecast?

CodimAI builds models for demand and sales forecasting, customer churn, payment and operational risk, and capacity planning. Any question with enough historical data and a clear decision attached is a candidate.

How accurate are AI forecasts?

Accuracy varies by problem and data quality, which is why every CodimAI model is backtested against real historical outcomes and ships with calibrated confidence ranges rather than a single false-certain number.

How much historical data do we need?

More history helps, but the right amount depends on the problem and how much it changes over time. The free audit assesses your data and tells you honestly whether a reliable model is feasible.

What does a confidence range actually mean?

Instead of a single number, a forecast ships with a range — for example a P10 to P90 band — that shows how certain the model is. A narrow band means high confidence; a wide one tells you to plan for variance rather than trust a point estimate.

Can forecasts integrate with our planning systems?

Yes. Predictions are delivered through an API and can surface in your ERP, inventory, CRM, or BI tools — so the forecast informs the decision where it is actually made.

Do the models stay accurate over time?

Conditions change, so forecasts drift. CodimAI monitors accuracy continuously and retrains models on fresh data to keep them dependable.
