// What it is

What is an email AI agent?

An email AI agent is an AI system that handles your inbound email on your behalf. It reads each message, works out what the sender actually wants, and sorts it, then drafts or sends an accurate, on-brand reply grounded in your real product, order, and policy information. When a message calls for it, the agent takes action: it looks up an order, updates a record, books a slot, or routes the email to the right person with the full thread attached.

Email is still where most serious business gets done, which is exactly why it piles up. Messages arrive constantly, in volume, and the majority are variations on the same handful of requests. A person working through them one by one is slow and expensive, and important emails get buried under routine ones. An agent reads and triages everything the moment it lands, and answers the routine majority without anyone lifting a finger.

CodimAI builds these agents on top of your existing mailbox, whether that is Gmail, Microsoft 365, or a shared helpdesk, and tunes each one to your brand and your business. The goal is not an auto-responder but a genuinely helpful teammate that clears the repetitive volume and surfaces the messages that need a human. The result is faster replies, a calmer inbox, and nothing important slipping through the cracks.

// Why email

The channel that never empties

Email is the default way businesses talk to customers, suppliers, and each other, and that is precisely why it never stops. Every enquiry, order, complaint, and follow-up lands in the same place, and the volume only grows. For most teams the inbox is a permanent backlog, with genuinely important messages sitting unread behind a wall of routine ones.

Working through it by hand does not scale. Replies slow down, threads get lost, and the same questions get answered again and again by people whose time is worth far more. A customer who emailed on Friday and heard nothing until Tuesday has often already moved on, and the cost of that slow reply rarely shows up on any report, but it is real.

An email agent changes the maths. It reads and sorts every message the instant it arrives, answers the routine majority, and pushes the rest to the right person already prioritised and summarised. Your team stops triaging and starts handling only what genuinely needs them. The inbox stops being a source of dread and becomes something that quietly stays under control.

And because the agent is grounded in your real data and connected to your systems, it does more than write polite replies. It resolves. A status request returns the real status. An invoice query is answered from the actual record. That is the difference between an auto-responder that stalls and an agent that genuinely closes the loop.

// Getting started

How to launch your email agent

Getting started is simpler than most teams expect. You do not need to migrate your email or rebuild your systems. You need your existing mailbox, the questions your senders actually ask, and the information needed to answer them, all of which you already have.

We begin with a free audit. We look at your real email to see what is repetitive and automatable, and we estimate the hours and cost an agent would save. From there we ground the agent in your content, connect it to your mailbox and systems, and set it live, usually within a few weeks.

You stay in control throughout. You decide, per category, whether the agent sends on its own, drafts for one-click approval, or simply triages and routes. We prove it on real emails before it touches a live reply, and once it is live we keep tuning it from actual messages so it gets sharper the longer it runs.

If you already run a shared inbox or helpdesk with a person or small team behind it, you are in the best possible position to start. The agent slots in alongside them, takes the repetitive volume off their plate, and escalates the rest, so nothing breaks and the load simply lightens. There is no rip-and-replace and no risky switch-over, just a calmer inbox and faster replies from day one.

// FAQ

Email AI agent questions

What is an email AI agent?

An email AI agent is an AI system that handles your inbound email automatically. It reads each message, works out the intent, sorts and prioritises it, drafts or sends an accurate reply in your brand voice, and takes actions like looking up an order or updating a record, escalating anything sensitive to a person with full context.

How much of our email can it automate?

Most teams automate 60 to 75 percent of routine email: common questions, status requests, form-style enquiries, and follow-ups. The remaining complex or sensitive messages are routed to the right person with the full thread attached.

Which email systems does it work with?

It works with Gmail and Google Workspace, Outlook and Microsoft 365, and shared helpdesks such as Zendesk, Freshdesk, and Help Scout. We connect to your existing mailbox rather than asking you to switch tools.

Can it connect to our CRM and other tools?

Yes. The agent integrates with your CRM, order and ticketing systems, and calendar so it can answer with real data, log every message, and take actions like updating a record or booking a slot.

Will it sound like our brand?

Yes. We tune the agent to your tone and rules and ground it in your real product, order, and policy information, so replies read like your best team member, not a generic template.

Can it draft replies for review instead of sending automatically?

Yes. You choose the level of autonomy per category. The agent can send routine replies on its own, draft replies for one-click approval, or simply triage and route, and you can change those rules at any time.

How long does it take to launch?

A focused email agent typically goes live in two to four weeks. We build a working prototype on your real inbox first so you can validate it before it touches a live reply.
