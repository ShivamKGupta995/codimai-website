// What it is

What is a WhatsApp AI agent?

A WhatsApp AI agent is an AI system that handles customer conversations on WhatsApp on your behalf. It reads each incoming message, works out what the customer actually wants, and responds with a helpful, accurate answer, drawing on your real product, order, and policy information. When a conversation calls for it, the agent takes action: it checks an order, books a slot, qualifies a lead, or passes the chat to a human with full context.

WhatsApp is where a huge share of customers now prefer to talk, which is exactly why it overwhelms teams. Messages arrive at all hours, in volume, and most of them are variations on the same handful of questions. A person answering them one by one is slow, expensive, and never off the clock. An agent answers instantly, every time, and never sleeps.

CodimAI builds these agents on the official WhatsApp Business Platform and tunes each one to your brand and your business. The goal is not a clunky menu bot but a genuinely helpful assistant that resolves the routine majority of conversations on its own and hands the rest to your team. The result is faster replies, lower cost, and customers who get answered the moment they reach out.

// Why WhatsApp

Meet customers where they already are

WhatsApp has quietly become the front door to many businesses. Customers would rather send a quick message than navigate a phone tree, wait on hold, or fill in a web form. That preference is a gift and a burden: it brings you closer to customers, but it buries your team under a flood of messages that all expect an instant, personal reply.

Hiring your way out of that does not scale. Volume spikes in the evenings and weekends, exactly when staff are not at their desks, so response times stretch and leads cool. The customer who messaged at 9pm and heard nothing until the next afternoon has often already bought elsewhere. The cost of slow replies on WhatsApp is rarely measured, but it is real.

An AI agent changes the economics. It answers the moment a message lands, whatever the hour, and handles the routine majority without a person. Your team stops drowning in the repetitive and focuses on the conversations that need judgement. Customers feel looked after, leads get caught while they are warm, and the channel that was a burden becomes one of your best.

And because the agent is grounded in your real data and connected to your systems, it does more than chat. It resolves. A status check returns the actual status. A booking request lands in the actual calendar. That is the difference between a bot that frustrates and an agent that genuinely helps.

// Getting started

How to launch your WhatsApp agent

Getting started is simpler than most teams expect. You do not need to reorganise your support team or rebuild your systems. You need a WhatsApp number, the questions your customers actually ask, and the information needed to answer them, all of which you already have.

We begin with a free audit. We look at your real conversations to see what is repetitive and automatable, and we estimate the hours and cost an agent would save. From there we ground the agent in your content, connect it to your systems, and set it live on the official WhatsApp Business Platform, usually within a few weeks.

You stay in control throughout. You decide what the agent handles alone, what it escalates, and how it sounds. We prove it on real cases before launch, and once it is live we keep tuning it from actual conversations so it gets better the longer it runs. Start with the audit, and see what your busiest channel looks like when nothing waits for a reply.

If you already run a WhatsApp number with a person or small team behind it, you are in the best possible position to start. The agent slots in alongside them, takes the repetitive volume off their plate, and escalates the rest, so nothing breaks and the load simply lightens. There is no rip-and-replace and no risky switch-over, just a quieter inbox and faster replies from day one.

// FAQ

WhatsApp AI agent questions

What is a WhatsApp AI agent?

A WhatsApp AI agent is an AI system that handles customer conversations on WhatsApp automatically. It reads each message, understands the intent, answers questions, qualifies leads, and completes actions like booking or order lookup, around the clock and in your brand voice, escalating to a human when needed.

How much of our WhatsApp chat can it automate?

Most businesses automate 70 to 80 percent of routine conversations: common questions, status checks, qualification, and bookings. The remaining complex or sensitive cases are handed to your team with full context.

Does it use the official WhatsApp Business API?

Yes. We build on the official WhatsApp Business Platform so your agent runs on a verified business number with reliable delivery and compliance, not an unofficial workaround.

Can it connect to our CRM and catalogue?

Yes. The agent integrates with your CRM, order system, catalogue, and calendar so it can answer with real data, log conversations, and take actions like booking a slot or checking an order.

Will it sound like our brand?

Yes. We tune the agent to your tone and rules and ground it in your real product and policy information, so replies are on brand and accurate, not generic.

How long does it take to launch?

A focused WhatsApp agent typically goes live in two to four weeks. We build a working prototype on your real content first so you can validate it before launch.

What happens for complex queries?

The agent recognises when a conversation needs a person and hands it over smoothly, passing the full context so your team can pick up without making the customer repeat themselves.
